Booking terms

How booking here works

Last updated: August 2026

Booking a chair

Picking a time online holds that slot for you with that barber. Nothing is charged online — you pay in the shop, cash or card, at the price shown when you booked.

Turning up

Get there a couple of minutes early. If you are more than ten minutes late we will do our best, but we may have to shorten the cut or move you to the next free slot so we do not hold up everybody behind you.

Changing or cancelling

Use the link in your confirmation email — it lets you move or cancel the booking yourself, up to 2 hours before. Inside that window, give the shop a ring and we will sort it. No charge either way. We just ask that you tell us, so somebody else can have the slot.

If we have to cancel

Illness and emergencies happen. If we have to move you we will ring or email as early as we can and get you back in at the first time that suits.

The cut card

  • Free to join, no card to carry.
  • Every paid cut gets a stamp when your barber marks it done.
  • Eight stamps and your next cut is on the house. The card then resets and starts again.
  • Free apprentice cuts do not earn a stamp — they are already free.
  • Stamps belong to the person, not the card number, and cannot be sold, swapped or exchanged for cash.
  • The card is a goodwill thing. We can change how it works or wind it up, but any stamps you have already earned will be honoured.

Free cuts with the apprentices

Ronnie, Owen and Lawrence are training under Mitch, which is why those cuts cost nothing. Mitch checks the work. They take the same care and the same time as any other cut — the difference is who is holding the clippers.

Under 16s

Anyone under 16 needs a parent or guardian with them. Please book the "Kids cut 12 and under" slot for younger ones so we set aside the right amount of time.

Common sense

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone abusive to the team or to other customers. That is the only rule we are strict about.
