Privacy

What we do with your details

Last updated: August 2026

Plain English, because you should not need a solicitor to work out what happens to your phone number.

Who we are

Mitch's Barbershop, 18 Grange Road, Ramsgate, Kent CT11 9LR. We are the data controller for everything described here. Get in touch at the shop or pop in.

What we collect, and why

  • When you book: your name, mobile number, email address, and anything you type in the notes box. We need these to hold the chair for you, to send your confirmation and reminder, and to ring you if something changes.
  • When you join the cut card: the same details, plus a running count of your stamps. That is the only way the card can work.
  • Basic technical information that any website receives — your browser type and IP address — handled by our hosting provider for security and to stop bots filling up the diary.

We do not use tracking cookies, we do not run advertising pixels, and we do not profile you.

What we do not collect

No card or payment details ever touch this website. You pay in the shop.

Why we are allowed to hold it

  • Your booking and its confirmation: we need it to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • The cut card and any offers: only because you ticked the box (consent). You can withdraw that at any time from the link at the bottom of any email we send, or from your own booking page.
  • Keeping records of cuts done: running the shop properly and keeping our books straight (legitimate interests).

Who else sees it

Only the companies that run the plumbing, and only so they can do their job:

  • Supabase — stores the diary and the card. Data held in the EU.
  • Resend — sends your confirmation, reminder and card emails.
  • Vercel — serves this website.

We do not sell your details, we do not swap them with other businesses, and we do not send them anywhere else.

How long we keep it

  • Booking history: up to two years, so we can see who is a regular.
  • Cut card and mailing list: until you ask us to remove you, or after three years with no cuts.
  • Sent emails: 60 days, then deleted automatically.

What you can ask us to do

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you. Just ask — in the shop, on the phone, or by email — and we will sort it within a month, free of charge. Your own booking page (the link in your confirmation email) already lets you cancel bookings and turn off offer emails yourself.

If you think we have got it wrong and we cannot put it right between us, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Keeping it safe

Customer details are held behind the database's own security rules — they are never included in the public web pages, and the website's public key cannot read them. Only signed-in staff accounts can see the diary and the card list.
